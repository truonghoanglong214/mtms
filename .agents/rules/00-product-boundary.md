# MTMS product boundary
- Internal multi-shop financial and operational management ONLY.
- Do not create POS, carts, individual drink orders, online payment gateways or automatic recipe stock consumption.
- Preserve daily aggregate revenue and separate actual bank funds from delivery-app net revenue.
- Never turn a proposed feature into approved business scope without a recorded BR decision.
